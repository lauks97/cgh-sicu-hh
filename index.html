⏳

Recommended duration for alcohol-based hand rub?

Survey Q3

✅

20–30 Seconds

Any less, and the alcohol doesn't have enough contact time to kill the bugs!

🖐️

Which of these belong to the WHO 5 Moments?

Survey Q4

ALL except "Before touching the computer"!

  • 1. Before touching patient
  • 2. Before aseptic procedure
  • 3. After body fluid exposure
  • 4. After touching patient
  • 5. After touching surroundings
🛡️

Hand hygiene is most important in preventing...

Survey Q5

🏥

Healthcare-Associated Infections (HAIs)

It prevents BOTH viral and bacterial cross-transmission in the SICU.

🧤

Can gloves replace hand hygiene?

Survey Q6

❌

NO!

Microscopic leaks exist, and hands often get contaminated during glove removal.

📊 CGH SICU Audit Insights

Real talk from Cycle 1: Why we introduced the new digital cue.

🤯

Forgetfulness Spiked

Staff citing "forgetfulness" as a barrier jumped from 26% to 75% by Week 3! High workload makes us run on autopilot.

❤️

Driven by Conscience

Up to 62.5% of you clean your hands because of your own conscience, NOT because of audits or strict seniors.

⚠️

The Moment 5 Blindspot

In real audits, compliance for Moment 5 (touching surroundings) dropped to 30.8%. We often forget that privacy curtains and bed rails carry patient bacteria!
